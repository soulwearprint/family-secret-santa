🎅 Family Secret Santa — how it works

🇭🇺 Magyar ← back to the app

Purpose and goal

Family Secret Santa is our family Secret Santa; in Hungarian it is called Pereputty Angyalkázás. Pereputty is the whole clan — in-laws, friends and all — and angyalkázás is the Hungarian name for the game of drawing names in secret. The app organises gift draws for this big, extended "family", which may well reach beyond relatives. Everyone draws one person to buy a present for, and nobody should know who drew them until the gifts are opened.

The app's goals, in order of importance:

  1. Keep the secret. Nobody — not even the people running the app — sees who drew whom unless an admin deliberately unlocks it, and every unlock is logged.
  2. Fair, random draws that respect the family's wishes: couples apart, no repeats of last year, fixed pairs for small children or grandparents, several draws side by side.
  3. Good presents without phoning around: everyone can list a few gift ideas within the budget, and their Secret Santa is kept up to date.
  4. Include everyone, also those without e-mail or who don't use the internet, through helpers.
  5. Make it feel festive: postcards from the North Pole, in Hungarian or English, with a fresh design each year.

1 · People: participants, helpers, groups

Participants

  • Everyone who can take part in a draw is a participant, with a name, an e-mail address and a postcard language (Hungarian by default, or English).
  • Removing someone who took part in a past draw archives them: their history stays, and they are left out of new draws. Someone who never took part is deleted. An archived person can be restored by editing them.

Helpers (people without e-mail)

Someone without an e-mail address must have a helper: another participant who has their own e-mail.
  • All of that person's mail — invitation, postcard, daily digest — goes to the helper, clearly marked "for <name>". The helper also manages their gift ideas through the link in that mail.
  • A helper can't remove their own e-mail address while they help someone, and a helper can't themselves need a helper.
  • A person never draws their own helper — in any draw. Why: the helper reads that person's postcard and would otherwise learn who their own Secret Santa is. This is the only draw rule that applies to every draw.

Groups

  • Groups are named, reusable lists of participants ("Family circle", "Cousins"). Anyone can be in any number of groups.
  • Groups are only a starting point: a draw copies the group's people when it is created, or with Quick add a group. Changing or deleting a group later doesn't change existing draws.

2 · Admins

  • Up to 5 admins. The first admin is created at setup, is mandatory and can't be removed; any admin can add or remove the others.
  • Admins sign in with a password, a Google account, or both. Google sign-in only accepts verified Google addresses that are registered as admins; others are refused and the attempt is logged.
  • A login lasts 30 minutes from the last action.
  • Admins manage participants, groups, draws, rules, designs and sending — but don't see who drew whom.
  • Unlocking the matches of a draw needs a fresh confirmation (typing the password again, or a new Google sign-in that is valid for one unlock within 2 minutes) and is always written to the activity log as MATCHES REVEALED, visible to all admins.
  • Every change (participants, rules, draws, designs, sending, gift-idea edits by admins) appears in the 📜 Log.
  • There is no password reset: the matches are encrypted with the admins' passwords, so keep a second admin as a backup.

3 · Draws

  • A draw is a calendar year plus an optional name, e.g. 2026 · Family circle, 2026 · Everyone, 2026 · Lucky 6. Any number of draws can run side by side; names are unique within a year.
  • Each draw has its own participants, matches, rules, fixed pairs, budget, gift-exchange date, note from Santa, card design, postcards, links, gift ideas and digests. Someone in two draws gets two postcards, and each names its draw.
  • Who takes part when a draw is created: the same people as last year's draw with the same name, everyone, a group, a random N people (from a group or from everyone), or nobody yet. The list can always be edited.
  • A new draw starts with the budget, note, rule switches, design and "never draw each other" pairs of last year's draw with the same name.
  • Deleting a draw removes its participants list, matches, links, gift ideas and rules. It can't be undone.

4 · Draw rules

Every draw follows these rules. If they can't all be met at once, the app refuses to draw and says so — it never quietly breaks a rule.

  1. One each. Everyone in the draw gives exactly one present and receives exactly one. Nobody draws themselves.
  2. Never draw each other (per draw). Pairs set on a draw's page can't draw each other, in either direction. They apply to that draw only: a pair can be excluded in one draw and allowed in another. Copy rules from another draw adds another draw's pairs on purpose; it never removes any.
  3. Not your own helper (every draw, see helpers).
  4. No repeat of last year (switch per draw, on by default). Nobody gets the same person as in last year's draw with the same name that was actually drawn.
  5. Not the same giftee in two draws (switch per draw, on by default). Nobody gets the same person as in another draw of the same calendar year that has already been drawn.
  6. Fixed pairs (per draw). An admin can set "A gives to B" by hand, e.g. Grandma → little Peti. A fixed pair wins over every other rule: A can only draw B, and nobody else can draw B. In a draw, each person can be the giver of at most one fixed pair and the receiver of at most one. Fixed pairs are visible to all admins.

Randomness: among all assignments that satisfy the rules, every one is equally likely. If random tries can't find one, an exhaustive search decides whether any valid assignment exists at all.

Rules or fixed pairs changed after a draw apply at the next reshuffle.

5 · Changing the list after a draw

  • If people are added to or removed from a draw that has been drawn, no postcards can be sent until an admin confirms who to reshuffle.
  • The admin chooses Reshuffle everyone (a completely new draw) or ticks the people who should get a new giftee. Everyone else keeps theirs — useful when presents are already bought.
  • Always included automatically: new people; people whose giftee left; people whose match now breaks a rule.
  • The last two groups are included without being shown, mixed with the same number of randomly chosen decoys (plus a replacement whenever the admin happens to tick one of them), so the reshuffle can't reveal who was giving to whom. The admin sees only a count.
  • If the chosen set still can't be solved, random extra people are added until it can.
  • Everyone who is reshuffled gets a fresh, marked "Updated card" — even if they happen to draw the same person again — so the "not sent" list doesn't give anything away. Their old link stops working.

6 · What participants receive

Every e-mail goes to one person only (no CC/BCC). Mail for someone without e-mail goes to their helper, marked "for <name>". When a draw has a name, it appears in the subject and on the card.

E-mailWhenWhat it contains
✉️ Wish-list invitation (optional) Before the draw, when an admin sends it. By default only to people who don't have a link yet; never replaces a postcard link. A private link to add gift ideas, and the gift budget. No match — nothing has been drawn.
📮 Postcard After the draw, when an admin sends it (all pending at once, or one person). Can be resent. Your giftee's name and their current gift ideas, budget, exchange date, Santa's note, a button to your private page, and a link to read the card in the other language. The subject never names the giftee, because subjects show on lock screens.
📬 Daily digest At most once a day (see digest). What changed on your giftee's wish list: new ideas, ideas no longer wanted (with the reason), the current list, the budget.

Links

  • Each person has one private link per draw. A postcard link replaces the invitation link; resending a card creates a new link and the old one stops working ("this link is no longer valid").
  • Anyone with the link sees that person's match — like the postcard itself — so links shouldn't be forwarded.

7 · The personal page (no login)

  • Shows the gift budget prominently, the gift-exchange date, the giftee and the giftee's gift ideas (ideas no longer wanted appear crossed out, with the reason).
  • Lets the person manage their own gift ideas (rules) and copy their ideas from another draw they take part in.
  • Lets them switch the postcard language, optionally getting the card sent again in the new language.
  • Before the draw (invitation link) it shows ideas only, with a note that the postcard will follow.
  • It follows the draw's card design.

8 · Gift ideas

  • Ideas belong to one person in one draw. Up to 5 ideas that are still wanted, each with an optional link.
  • An idea that the person's Secret Santa hasn't received yet (not on their postcard or in a digest) can simply be removed — nobody hears about it.
  • Once received, an idea can't be deleted; it can only be marked no longer wanted, with a reason: already got it / have a similar one, don't want it any more, added by mistake, or other. The Secret Santa is told in the next digest.
  • A crossed-out idea can be restored. If the Secret Santa had already been told it was no longer wanted, it comes back to them as new.
  • Copying from another draw brings over only ideas that are still wanted, skips ones already on the list (ignoring upper/lower case), respects the limit of 5, and counts as new for this draw's Secret Santa.
  • Admins can edit anyone's ideas (e.g. for small children or grandparents) with exactly the same rules; every edit is logged.
  • If someone's Secret Santa changes in a reshuffle, the new one has seen nothing yet: their ideas count as not received again, and crossed-out ones are dropped.

9 · The daily digest

  • Changes to someone's ideas are collected and sent to their Secret Santa in one e-mail per day, after 18:00 Budapest time by default.
  • Only after that Secret Santa's postcard has gone out (until then, the postcard itself will list the ideas).
  • It never repeats something the Secret Santa has already been told. If nothing changed, nothing is sent.
  • After a reshuffle, it goes to the person's new Secret Santa.
  • Admins can see how many updates are waiting and press Send digest now.

10 · Card design and language

  • Each draw has its own design: base theme, accent colour, font, theme animal (optionally in a Santa hat) and decorations, previewed before saving. Postcards, invitations, digests and the personal page all follow it.
  • Postcards are in Hungarian by default; each participant can be set to English, by an admin or by themselves.
  • Texts written by admins (budget, exchange date, Santa's note, draw name) are shown exactly as typed, in every language.

11 · Secrecy and privacy

  • Matches are stored encrypted. The database file alone doesn't reveal who drew whom. (Digest e-mails waiting to be sent are protected by a separate server key; keep that key outside the data folder, as the README describes.)
  • Sending postcards and digests doesn't show the content to the admin who presses the button.
  • Private links are stored only as fingerprints; the server can't recreate them.
  • To address a digest, the server can find a person's Secret Santa only at the moment that person (or an admin) changes their ideas; it is stored encrypted until the digest goes out.
  • Gift ideas, participant names and e-mail addresses are not secret and not encrypted.

12 · Limits worth knowing

  • Copies of e-mails: a sending account's "Sent" folder, or an e-mail service's log (e.g. Resend), contains every postcard. Only people who shouldn't know the matches must not have access to it.
  • Shared inboxes and helpers see the cards they receive — by design for helpers.
  • Small draws: with very few people, the reshuffle decoys give less cover; with 3–4 people it can be guessed.
  • Images in e-mails load from the app's address; some mail apps show them only after "show images".
  • Email apps differ: Outlook simplifies the card's border; Gmail on Android uses its own fonts.

13 · Admin panel language

  • The admin panel can be switched between Hungarian and English with the HU | EN switch in the header. The browser remembers the choice; the first time, it follows the browser's language.
  • It only affects the admin panel and this guide. The language of participants' e-mails is set per participant (card design and language).
  • Log entries are stored with English keywords (e.g. MATCHES REVEALED); the Hungarian panel shows them translated.
